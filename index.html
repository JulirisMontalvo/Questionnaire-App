How the Sorting Hat Works

1. Read each of the 10 magical questions carefully.

2. Choose the answer that best reflects your true personality.

3. Each choice gives points to one of the four Hogwarts houses: Gryffindor, Ravenclaw, Hufflepuff, or Slytherin.

4. At the end, the Sorting Hat reveals your official house!
